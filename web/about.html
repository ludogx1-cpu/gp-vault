What is Golden Paw?

You adopt a baby Shiba, give it a name and watch it grow through 8 life stages, from a sleepy baby to a wise old dog. Feed it, play fetch, go on walks and collect collars along the way.

The Book Of Dog is where you share your real dog: post a photo, give likes and leave kind comments. It's dogs only, with a Report button on every post and comment, and location data is removed from photos automatically.

Sign in with your Google account or email to save your progress on any device. Signing up is free: no card and no deposits.

GPAWS and optional DOGE rewards

GPAWS are our loyalty points. Golden paws appear around the app (up to 5 an hour) and you tap them to collect. They have no cash value.

Some optional extras for players aged 18 and over involve small Dogecoin (DOGE) rewards: a small reward you can collect every 5 minutes, and the Bonus Vault, a just-for-fun game feature where a small bonus builds up on DOGE you've collected. You never need to deposit anything, and you can enjoy Golden Paw without them.

Golden Paw is entertainment, not an investment or a way to make money. It is not a financial product, is not authorised by the FCA, and does not take deposits. Rewards are small and not guaranteed, and the value of DOGE can go down as well as up. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
