✦ HELP & SUPPORT ✦

How can we help?

Questions about an order, a download, a website project or the Golden Paw app? You'll find the answers here, or email us and a real person will reply.

📧 Contact us

Email us any time. We reply within 24 hours, Monday to Friday, and usually much sooner.

goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com Email support

If it's about an order, include the receipt number from your Stripe email. It helps us find it quickly.

🛒 Orders & payments

  • Payments are taken securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • You'll get a receipt by email straight after paying. On your bank statement the payment shows as GOLDENPAW.
  • Didn't get a receipt? Check your spam folder, then email us.

📥 Digital downloads

  • Downloads are instant: after paying you're taken straight to your file. Save it to your device. Lost it? Email us with your Stripe receipt and we'll resend it.
  • Printables are A4 and work in colour or black and white. Choose "Actual size" or "100%" when printing.
  • Files are for your personal use. Please don't share or resell them.
  • The Groomer & Dog Walker Starter Kit includes a business licence: type in your business name and use it with all your own clients (one business). Just don't resell or share the files.

🛠️ Website & service projects

  • Audits are delivered within 24 hours of payment.
  • Websites: you see a free preview first and only pay once you're happy with it.
  • Chrome extensions: we confirm your brief by email before we start, and send updates as we go.
  • Changing your mind before we start work? Just tell us and you'll get a full refund.

🔁 Monthly plans (care plan & social pack)

  • Cancel any time. Email us and we'll stop it before your next payment.
  • Cancelling stops future payments. The current month runs to the end of its period.
  • To change your card details, email us and we'll send you a secure link.

↩️ Refunds

  • If something isn't right, a faulty file or work that isn't as described, email us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you.
  • Because downloads are delivered digitally, we can't usually refund a download once it's been sent, unless it's faulty or not as described.
  • Services we haven't started yet are fully refundable.

None of this affects your statutory rights as a UK consumer.

🐾 Golden Paw app & Chrome extension

  • Trouble signing in, a missing reward or a bug? Email us with your username and what happened.
