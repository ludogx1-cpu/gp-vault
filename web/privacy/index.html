Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Short version: we collect what we need to run your account and pay your rewards, we use a captcha to stop bots, and we only use analytics or advertising cookies if you agree to them. You can change your cookie choices at any time, and you can ask us to see or delete your data by emailing goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Golden Paw (goldenpaw.dog, the Golden Paw Android app and the Golden Paw Chrome extension) is an 18+ entertainment platform with a virtual pet, games and small Dogecoin rewards. Golden Paw is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the website, the Android app and the Chrome extension. It replaces all earlier versions, including the separate cookie policy.

2. Age requirement

Golden Paw is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

On the website we use a cookie banner (provided by Cloudflare Zaraz) that asks for your choices before any analytics or advertising cookies are set. Until you choose, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and AdRoll are not loaded, and Google tags run in "consent denied" mode, which means they set no cookies.

Strictly necessary (always on)

  • Login session (Google Firebase, local storage) keeps you signed in.
  • App settings (local storage) remember things like dark mode, your saved payout address and timers.
  • Security and captcha (Cloudflare, Cloudflare Turnstile, hCaptcha, Google reCAPTCHA / Firebase App Check) protect the site from bots and attacks.
  • Your cookie choices (Cloudflare Zaraz) remember what you picked in the cookie banner.

Analytics (only with your consent)

  • Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): cookies such as _ga, _ga_* and _gcl_au.

Advertising (only with your consent where required)

  • AdRoll / NextRoll: retargeting cookies. Only loaded if you agree to personalised advertising.
  • Display ad networks that may appear on some pages: Google AdSense, Adsterra and BitcoTasks. Google ads follow your consent choice and show limited, non-personalised ads if you do not consent. Other networks may still set their own cookies when their ad loads; please see their policies below.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run Golden Paw. They process it for us or under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Firebase, Google Cloud, Google Analytics, AdSense, reCAPTCHA): login, database, hosting, push notifications, analytics, ads and bot protection. Policy
  • Render: hosts our reward server. Policy
  • Cloudflare: content delivery, security, Turnstile captcha and the cookie banner. Policy
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines): captcha. Policy
  • FaucetPay: receives your payout address and the amount when you claim or withdraw. Policy
  • AdRoll / NextRoll: retargeting ads, with your consent. Policy
  • Adsterra and BitcoTasks: display adverts and tasks. Adsterra policy
  • Offerwall partners (such as TimeWall and BitcoTasks): if you choose to complete an offer, the partner receives your Golden Paw user ID so it can credit you, and collects data under its own policy.
  • Trustpilot, YouTube, Tenor and Substack: embedded widgets that may load content from those services.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect our users or the service from fraud, or as part of a sale or transfer of the service (we would tell you first).

6. International transfers

Some of these providers store or process data outside the UK and European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where this happens we rely on adequacy decisions (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the UK extension to it) or the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, game and reward data: while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Payment and withdrawal records: up to 6 years after the payment, where we need them for legal, tax or fraud reasons.
  • Chat messages and suggestions: until you or we delete them, or your account is deleted.
  • Server and security logs: up to 90 days.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Cookies: as described above; most analytics cookies last up to 2 years unless you clear them or withdraw consent.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UK, EU and California), you have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your account and data;
  • object to or restrict how we use your data;
  • take your data to another service (portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example with the "Change cookie settings" button above, without affecting earlier use;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising (we do not sell data; you can refuse advertising cookies in the cookie banner).

To use any of these rights, email goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will reply within one month. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

9. Deleting your account

Email goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Golden Paw account", from the email address on your account. Please withdraw any balance you want to keep first, because unclaimed bonus balances are removed when the account is deleted.

10. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Database access is limited by security rules so that you can only read your own account data, and reward balances can only be changed by our server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires.

11. Chrome extension

The Golden Paw Chrome extension stores your sign-in token and settings in Chrome's extension storage on your device. It talks only to Golden Paw, Google sign-in and the captcha providers. It does not read the pages you visit or your browsing history.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will let you know in the app or by email.
