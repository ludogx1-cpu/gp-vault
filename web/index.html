Questions

Is Golden Paw free?

Yes. Signing up, raising your Shiba and using The Book Of Dog are all free. There are no deposits.

What are GPAWS?

GPAWS are our loyalty points. Golden paws appear around the app (up to 5 an hour) and you tap them to collect. They have no cash value.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. Golden Paw is a virtual pet game and a dog-lover community first. Some optional extras for players aged 18+ involve small Dogecoin rewards, but they are entertainment, not an investment, and you never need to deposit anything.

Who can join?

Golden Paw is for adults aged 18 and over.

Is my data safe?

We only collect what we need to run your account, we never sell your data, and we only use analytics or advertising cookies if you agree. You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Read our privacy policy.

Who's behind Golden Paw?

Golden Paw is made by Golden Paw Studios, a small independent studio in the UK. We're dog lovers who make games, printables and websites, and free tools like the Golden Paw Animation Creation Tool. We read every message: visit our support page or email goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com.
