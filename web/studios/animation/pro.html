Questions

Is it really a one-off payment?

Yes. You pay once and keep Pro, including future Pro updates. There's no subscription and nothing renews.

How many devices can I use it on?

Your licence key works on up to 3 of your own devices or browsers. You can deactivate one here to move it to another.

Does the AI upload my videos?

No. The AI runs on your own computer or phone, inside your browser. Your files never leave your device, just like the rest of the tool.

What do I need for the AI?

Any modern browser works. Chrome or Edge on a computer with a graphics card is fastest. The first time you use it, the AI downloads once (about 46 MB) and is then saved for next time.

Windows says "unknown publisher" when I install the desktop app

That's normal for a new small developer's app. Click More info → Run anyway. The app only runs the tool on your computer. You can also keep using Pro in your browser instead.
