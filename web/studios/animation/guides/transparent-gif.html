How to make a GIF with a transparent background

Animated GIFs work almost everywhere: chat apps, forums, emails and websites. They can be transparent too, as long as you understand one quirk of the format.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and load your video.
  2. Set the start and end of the part you want, and a frame rate (10 to 15 FPS suits most GIFs).
  3. Remove the background: tick Remove Background (Chroma Key) for a green screen or solid backdrop, or use AI background removal with Pro for any background.
  4. Under outputs, tick Animated GIF.
  5. Click Process File, check the preview, then download animation.gif.

Why GIF transparency is different

PNG and WebP store a smooth level of transparency for every pixel, so soft edges fade nicely into whatever is behind them. GIF can only make a pixel fully transparent or fully solid. There's no in-between.

The tool handles this for you: every pixel that's at least half visible becomes solid and the rest become transparent. It also picks a transparency colour that doesn't appear anywhere in your artwork, so none of your character disappears by accident.

Getting cleaner edges

  • Use crisp edges. Soft, feathered edges are what turn jagged in a GIF. Lower the chroma key's edge feathering, or choose Crisp edges for AI background removal.
  • Turn spill suppression up. Any green fringe left on the edge becomes a solid green outline in a GIF.
  • Think about where it will be shown. Edges look best on a background with a similar brightness to the one the clip was filmed on.

Keeping the file small

  • Lower the Output Scale. Halving the size makes the file roughly four times smaller.
  • Use fewer frames: a lower FPS or a shorter loop.
  • Trim transparent padding crops away empty space around the subject.
  • GIFs are limited to 256 colours per frame, so simple, flat-coloured artwork compresses far better than detailed video.

When not to use a GIF

For a game or an app, use the sprite sheet (PNG or WebP) instead. It keeps smooth edges and full colour, and is usually smaller. Use the GIF for sharing.
