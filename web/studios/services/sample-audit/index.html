A worked example for a typical small dog-grooming business, so you know exactly what you'll get before you order.

📋 Summary

Business: Muddy Paws Grooming, a one-person dog groomer in a market town.
Goal: more bookings from people searching "dog groomer near me".

The site looks friendly, but it's slow on phones, Google can't tell what town it serves, and there's no easy way to book. The good news: the five fixes below are quick and mostly free.

📍 Google Business Profile

  • Profile exists, but opening hours are out of date and there's no website link.
  • 14 reviews (4.8★), but none have replies. Replying shows customers you care, and helps your ranking.
  • Only one photo. Profiles with recent photos get more clicks.

🏆 Your top 5 fixes, in plain English

  1. Shrink the gallery photos to under 300 KB each (a free tool like Squoosh does it in a minute). Expected mobile score: 70+.
  2. Change the page title to "Dog Grooming in [Town] | Muddy Paws Grooming" and add a one-line description.
  3. Add a "Book now" button at the top of the page that opens your booking form, WhatsApp or phone.
  4. Mention your town and services in the main heading and first paragraph: "Friendly dog grooming in [Town]: full grooms, puppy trims and nail clips."
  5. Update your Google Business Profile: correct the hours, add the website link, add 5 recent photos and reply to every review.
