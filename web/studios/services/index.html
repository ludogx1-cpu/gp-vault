FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We handle the technical side and explain anything you need to know in plain English.

What about hosting and my domain name?

If you already own a domain, we connect it. If not, we help you buy one (usually around £10–£15 a year, paid directly to the registrar so it stays in your name). Hosting is included in the optional £19/month care plan, or we can set it up on your own account.

What does the £19/month care plan include?

Hosting, small text and photo changes, and monitoring that alerts us if your site goes down or slows. Cancel any time.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We use AI tools to work faster, which is how we keep prices low. Every piece of work is checked by a person before it reaches you.

How do I pay?

By secure card payment link once you've approved the preview. For larger jobs we may ask for a 50% deposit.
