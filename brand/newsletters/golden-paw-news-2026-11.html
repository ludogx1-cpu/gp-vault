Golden Paw News: November 2026

A golden glow-up, a brand-new Shiba and The Book Of Dog

Hello, dog lover! 🐾

It's been a busy six weeks at Golden Paw. We've given the whole site a golden makeover, drawn a brand-new Shiba for every stage of life, cut right back on adverts, and opened The Book Of Dog, our new home for real dogs. Here's everything that's new.

📖 The Book Of Dog is open

Our biggest addition yet. The Book Of Dog is a friendly feed where you share photos of your dog with other dog lovers. Post a photo with a caption, give likes, and leave kind comments.

We've built it to be safe and friendly from day one:

  • Dogs only, with clear Book Of Dog rules
  • A Report button on every post and comment, and anything reported by several people is hidden straight away while we check it
  • You can hide anyone's posts with one tap
  • Location data is removed from photos automatically

You'll find it in the new Book Of Dog tab at the bottom of the screen. Go and say hello to Goldie, our welcome pup, then add your own dog: goldenpaw.dog/book-of-dog

🐕 Your Shiba has had a glow-up

Your virtual Shiba has brand-new, hand-drawn artwork for every stage of life: baby, toddler, puppy, child, teen, young adult, adult and wise old dog. Every stage now has its own picture, and the eyes, blinks and collars are lined up perfectly on each one.

There's more for your pup, too:

  • No more egg: new players now meet their baby Shiba straight away
  • 9 collars to collect and wear
  • Livelier animations: breathing, little reactions, and a wandering Shiba that looks more 3D as it moves around your screen
  • A smoother game of fetch, with better throws and catches
  • The pet screens now match the rest of Golden Paw in our rich gold colours

✨ A fresh, golden look

  • A new solid-gold paw logo
  • A rich gold colour across the whole site, replacing the old yellow
  • A modern new font for the Golden Paw title
  • A proper light mode: the header, sidebar and bottom bar now turn light grey when you switch to light mode

🧹 Fewer adverts, cleaner pages

You told us there were too many adverts, and we listened. We've removed the pop-ups and pop-unders, the big banner adverts, and the bonus sponsor pages. Your Account page is now completely advert-free.

When you first visit, you'll now see Golden Paw straight away behind the age check, instead of a blank screen.

📝 Articles for dog lovers

Our blog posts now have their own pages that are easy to read and share. Find them all at goldenpaw.dog/articles, and tell us what you'd like us to write about next.

🛍️ The Golden Paw shop

Our shop is open at goldenpaw.dog/shop, with instant downloads:

  • Dog Care Planner: vet visits, vaccinations, flea and worming dates, feeding and training notes, all in one printable planner
  • Groomer & Dog Walker Starter Kit: ready-made paperwork for anyone starting a dog business

Run a small business? Golden Paw Studios also builds websites and does website check-ups: goldenpaw.dog/studios/services

Need help with anything? Our help page is at goldenpaw.dog/support.

💻 Golden Paw in your browser

The Golden Paw Chrome extension puts your Shiba in your browser toolbar. Check on your pet, feed it and play with it in one click, and get a nudge when it needs you. Search "Golden Paw" in the Chrome Web Store.

🔒 Safer and more private

  • Clearer cookie and analytics choices: nothing tracks you unless you say yes
  • Better spam and bot protection
  • Easier one-click unsubscribe links in our emails

🔮 Coming soon

We're already working on the next big update to The Book Of Dog: a profile for your dog, following your favourite dogs, sharing posts, replies to comments, and notifications. After that, short dog videos. We'll let you know as soon as they're ready.
