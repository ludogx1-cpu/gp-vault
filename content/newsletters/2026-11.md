- Dogs only, with clear Book Of Dog rules

You'll find it in the new **Book Of Dog** tab at the bottom of the screen. Go and say hello to Goldie, our welcome pup, then add your own dog: [goldenpaw.dog/book-of-dog](https://goldenpaw.dog/book-of-dog)
